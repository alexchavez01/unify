
# Usability Study Script for *Unify* Platform

**Introduction & Explanation**

Hello, and thank you for participating in this usability study. My name is [Facilitator Name], and I will be guiding you through this session today. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the usability of the *Unify* platform, a social networking and academic resource platform designed to connect students based on their academic and extracurricular interests.

During this session, you will be asked to complete several tasks while interacting with the platform. Your goal is to complete the tasks as naturally as possible, and I encourage you to think aloud as you navigate the platform. This means that you should verbalize your thoughts, such as what you're trying to do, what you're expecting to happen, and anything you notice, whether positive or negative.

Please remember that we are testing the platform, not you. If you encounter any challenges or confusion, it's not your fault; we're just looking for ways to improve the experience.

**Pre-test Questions**

1. Have you used similar platforms before (e.g., student networking platforms, academic resources websites)?
2. What is your primary use for a platform like *Unify*?
3. Do you prefer using a desktop or mobile device for academic networking and resources? Why?

---

**Home Page (Main Screen) Tour**

I will now give you a brief overview of the platform. This is the home page, where you can access various features such as:

- **Networking**: A section where you can connect with students who share your academic and extracurricular interests.
- **Search**: Use this to search for students, resources, or dorms.
- **ARE (Academic Resources Exchange)**: A marketplace for buying, selling, or exchanging academic resources.
- **Dorms**: Explore available dorm listings and find a room that meets your preferences.
- **Scotty AI**: A virtual assistant that can give academic advice and event updates.

Please take a moment to familiarize yourself with the layout. You can explore these features during the tasks.

---

**Task Scenarios**

### Task 1: Use the student networking and matching system to find a peer with similar interests.

**Scenario:**  
You’ve just joined *Unify* and want to find someone to study with who shares your major (Biology), is also interested in medical school, and plays intramural basketball. Use the matching feature to locate a student who meets these criteria and send them a message.

- Please take your time and explain what you're doing as you try to complete this task.

### Task 2: Use advanced filtering to discover a study group for your major.

**Scenario:**  
You’re a computer science major interested in cybersecurity. You want to find a study group or peers with similar career goals. Use the search and filtering system to locate at least two students with these shared interests.

- Please take your time and let me know what you find as you go through this process.

### Task 3: List and browse items in the academic resource marketplace.

**Scenario:**  
You want to sell your old “Data Structures and Algorithms” textbook and look for materials for your upcoming statistics course. Navigate the resource exchange to post your book and search for statistics-related resources.

- Let me know if you encounter any challenges in completing this task.

### Task 4: Find and favorite a dorm listing that suits your preferences.

**Scenario:**  
You’re planning to move next semester and want to explore available dorm rooms. Use the dorm listing feature to find a room with private bathrooms, view its details, and favorite it so you can come back to it later.

- Please walk me through your steps as you explore the dorm listings and mark your favorite.

### Task 5: Ask Scotty AI for academic advice and event updates.

**Scenario:**  
You’re unsure about which electives to take next semester. Ask Scotty AI for course recommendations based on your interest in creative writing and computer science. Then, inquire about upcoming campus events related to professional development.

- After interacting with Scotty AI, let me know how easy or difficult it was to find the information you were looking for.
