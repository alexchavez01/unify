# Usability Study Observation Notes – Observer: Alex

## Task 1: Use the student networking and matching system

**Observations:**
- Participant found the matching system easily from the navigation bar.
- However, they paused at the filtering section and asked
- They were unsure whether the search updated dynamically or needed manual input (hitting Enter or clicking a filter button).


## Task 2: Use advanced filtering to find a study group

**Observations:**
- Participant found the filtering system again but commented, “I don’t know what all these categories mean.”
- They also tried searching but weren’t sure if filtering affected the search results.



## Task 3: Post and browse items in the Academic Resource Exchange

**Observations:**
- Participant was able to list a book fairly easily, though they had to guess where the “post item” button was.
- They liked the simple layout but expected images for listings and clearer categories.
- Found it difficult to search for a specific item, especially since search bar seemed generic.

## Task 4: Favorite a dorm listing

**Observations:**

- Said they were hesitant to click on listings that looked empty.
- The lack of visual cues made the dorm section feel untrustworthy or incomplete.
- No color codes, icons, or tags to indicate.


## Task 5: Ask Scotty AI for course advice and event updates

**Observations:**
- Participant was intrigued by the “Scotty AI” section but didn't know where to type their query.
- Expressed interest in the idea of an AI assistant but wanted clearer instructions or a sample question to get started.


## General Notes

**Navigation Feedback:**
- Participant generally liked the layout and modern design.
- Navigation bar was clear, but there was no indication of the current active page.
- Wanted hover animations or visual responses for more intuitive movement.

**Visual Design Comments:**
- Homepage lacked engaging content—too much empty space and plain text.
- User suggested clearer CTAs like "Start Here" or "Join a Study Group."
